# Newsjacking Brief template

```markdown
# Newsjacking Brief · [YYYY-MM-DD]

Window: last 72 hours [widened to 7 days: yes / no]
Built from: [ICP Brief dated YYYY-MM-DD / quick intake]
Stories checked: [N] · Worth posting: [N]

## Post today

### [Headline in plain words]
- **Date:** [publish date] · **Source:** [link] · **Confidence:** Confirmed / Single source / Developing
- **What happened:** [2 to 3 sentences]
- **Why your buyer cares:** [tie to a named pain or trigger from the ICP Brief]
- **Angles you could take:**
  1. *[Contrarian]* [one sentence position]
  2. *[What this means for you]* [one sentence position]
  3. *[Prediction or first-hand]* [one sentence position]
- **Your take (answer these, then write):**
  - [question]
  - [question]
- **Where the conversation is:** [most visible post or person already talking about it, if found]
- **Window:** [e.g. about 24 hours / a few days]

## Worth a post this week

[same structure, can be shorter]

## Logged, not worth a post

- [Headline] · [date] · [link] · [one line on why it's logged, not posted]

## Not news anymore, still useful

- [Older story that's still a good commentary topic] · [date] · [link]

## What I searched

- Buckets covered: industry, buyer's world, competitors, big tech and AI, argument of the week
- Came up empty: [list]
- Notes: [anything the user should know, e.g. a story that looked big but couldn't be confirmed]
```
